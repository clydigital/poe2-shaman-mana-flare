MANA FLARE / ENTANGLE SHAMAN

Mana Flare /
Entangle Shaman.

Stack Mana, use Entangle / Frost Darts / Orb of Storms to create reliable crit-trigger events, and keep enough recovery and Rage to make every Mana Flare matter. The guide explains the build; the planner and simulator are there when you want to test a change.

How the build works.

Entangle, Frost Darts and Orb of Storms create crit events. Mana Flare is the large triggered hit. Maximum Mana improves Archmage and Mana-scaling damage; current Mana sets Mana Flare's own base hit. Mana regeneration then does three jobs: refills the next Flare, keeps defensive Mana healthy, and—through Furious Wellspring—helps Rage regeneration.

Build rule: Mana is the ceiling, but recovery decides how much of that ceiling you actually use. CDR is only good after the next Flare still has enough current Mana and Rage behind it.

Current build priorities

  1. Keep raising Mana through flat Mana, Intelligence and high local ES converted by EB.
  2. Keep Mana regeneration high enough that Flare frequency does not collapse the pool.
  3. Preserve Rage for Druidic Champion; do not use Rageforged again until the simulator shows a positive Rage budget.
  4. Cap elemental resistances and add Armour / partial mana-before-life so enemy hits do not empty the same resource used for damage.
  5. At endgame, compare Rathpith Flare against high-level Entangle as whole-character routes, not isolated tooltips.

Core skills and why they are here

E

Entangle

Default mapping trigger. With enough gem levels it changes role and becomes a serious native-damage skill between Flare cooldowns.

F

Frost Darts

Controlled boss trigger. Use enough crit to trigger reliably; do not overspend on cast speed if CDR/recovery is already the cap.

O

Orb of Storms

Persistent crit fishing. Rage-efficient because one cast keeps generating hits without repeatedly paying Furious Wellspring's +5 Rage skill cost.

M

Mana Flare

Main triggered nuke. Consumes 25% of current Mana, so opening-hit damage and sustained boss damage are different numbers.

A

Archmage

Core reservation. Maximum Mana feeds extra Lightning damage, so the same stat that scales Flare base also scales another damage layer.

R

Eternal Rage

100-Spirit persistent skill that adds Rage regeneration. Strong once Spirit lets you run it without removing Archmage; it stabilises Druidic Champion and prevents Rage starvation.

SKILLS

Recommended skill package.

E

Entangle

Mapping/default: Mana Flare · Pinpoint Critical · Branching Fissures II · Magnified Area · Accelerated Growth.

High-level route: if you push Entangle toward ~31–36, native Entangle damage becomes worth modelling as a second main engine.

F

Frost Darts

Controlled boss trigger. Mana Flare · Pinpoint Critical · Concentrated Area · Multishot-style efficiency. Rapid Casting is lower value once recovery or Flare cooldown is already the bottleneck.

O

Orb of Storms

Persistent crit fishing. It is Rage-efficient because you pay the skill event once and let the field keep generating hits instead of repeatedly paying +5 Rage for casts.

M

Mana Flare

Main triggered nuke. It consumes 25% of current Mana, which is why the damage calculator tracks current-Mana percentage separately from maximum Mana.

A

Archmage

Core Spirit reservation. Maximum Mana directly improves the extra Lightning contribution, so it remains one of the reasons Mana stacking is so efficient.

R

Mana Remnants

Not just convenience. Arcane Remnants, Empowering Remnants and pickup/extra-remnant nodes can become part of the actual boss recovery budget.

ETERNAL RAGE

What it does in this build.

Why Rage matters

Druidic Champion turns every 2 Rage into 1% more Spell Damage. With ~43 Rage, holding a full bar is roughly a 21.5% more-spell layer in the simplified model.

Furious Wellspring

It gives the build its unusual Rage economy: Mana-regeneration-rate scaling also applies to Rage regeneration. That means a Mana regen node can support Flare recovery and Rage at the same time.

Eternal Rage

A persistent 100-Spirit skill that adds direct Rage regeneration. It becomes strong when you can fit it without removing Archmage. Until then, fix Rage with regen and a more efficient rotation first.

Illustrative 43-Rage Atlas state: Furious base = 43 × 6% = 2.58 Rage/s With +250% Mana regen scaling → 2.58 × 3.5 ≈ 9.03 Rage/s Add an Atlas-level Eternal Rage around ~4.25 Rage/s → ~13.28 Rage/s simplified supply If your paid events are ~1 manual cast + 1.5 Flare triggers / sec: 2.5 events × 5 Rage ≈ 12.5 Rage/s demand Result: roughly Rage-neutral / slightly positive.
Rageforged II stays out of the default build. Your live test is more important than its tooltip: when it drains Rage enough to stop Mana Flare triggering, the real multiplier is negative.
ROUTES

Where the planner is meant to take you.

Balanced Mana / armour / Ward

Lowest-friction route. High local ES → EB Mana, good regen, capped resists, armour and incidental Runic Ward. Keep MoM while levelling, then test partial mana-before-life without its recovery penalty.

Rathpith Mana Flare

8–10k+ Mana, strong crit, strong CDB, cultivated Rathpith and recovery-matched CDR. Best pure Flare boss ceiling if the pool can actually refill between procs.

L31–36 Entangle hybrid

Use +spell/gem-level gear to make Entangle itself deal serious damage between Flare cooldowns. The calculator lets you compare the Mana loss from giving up Rathpith against the native Entangle route.
