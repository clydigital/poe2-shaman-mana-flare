MAIN BUILD IDEA

Trigger → Scale → Refill

The build is simple when stripped down. Crit often, make each Flare enormous, then recover fast enough that the next Flare is still enormous.

1 · Trigger

Entangle for mapping. Frost Darts for bosses. The carrier crits and triggers Mana Flare.

2 · Scale

Maximum Mana scales Archmage and Mana-based damage. Current Mana determines Mana Flare's actual base hit.

3 · Refill

Mana regen, Mana Remnants, flasks and cost efficiency rebuild current Mana between Flares and also help maintain Rage.

CORE

Archmage + Mana Remnants

The permanent engine. Archmage turns maximum Mana into damage; Remnants help restore and overflow current Mana.

STABLE FLEX

Eternal Rage

Same 100-Spirit cost as CoC, but much easier to sustain. Keeps Druidic Champion and Rage scaling stable.

PREMIUM FLEX

Cast on Critical → Comet

Higher ceiling, but the real cost is recovery. Triggered Comets are only worth it once they stop dragging down Mana Flare's current-Mana payload.

MAIN ITEMS TO CONSIDER

What each slot is trying to do

There are two legitimate endgame chest routes: Morior for socket/defence value, or a high-ES + %Mana rare for maximum raw Mana. Everything else should support Mana, recovery, crit or the socket/Bonded engine.

Jiquani rule: huge pure-ES helmet + Eldritch Battery is already good. Jiquani can effectively make that helmet ES contribute again as flat Mana through the premium Darkness Enthroned interaction. Treat it as aspirational BIS, not a requirement.

CDR rule: more cooldown recovery is only damage when your Mana recovery can feed the extra Flare rate. Otherwise it just empties the pool faster.
