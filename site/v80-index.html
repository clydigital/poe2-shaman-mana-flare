MAIN BUILD IDEA

Trigger. Scale. Refill.

The build functions as a loop. Entangle or Frost Darts supplies the trigger, Mana supplies the damage, and regen, remnants and flasks rebuild current Mana before the next trigger cycle.

1 · Trigger

Entangle handles mapping through wide, persistent coverage. Frost Darts carries boss damage with a tighter support package.

2 · Scale

Maximum Mana scales Archmage, Mana-based damage and defensive depth. Current Mana defines Mana Flare's actual base hit.

3 · Refill

Mana regeneration, Mana Remnants, cost efficiency and flask support restore current Mana between casts and sustain the Rage version cleanly.

CORE ENGINE

Archmage + Mana Remnants

The permanent foundation. Archmage converts a large Mana pool into damage while Remnants reinforce recovery and overflow.

STABLE VERSION

Eternal Rage

The default 100-Spirit flex. Rage generation is steady, Druidic Champion remains active, and sustain pressure stays reasonable.

PREMIUM VERSION

Cast on Critical → Comet

The higher ceiling package. It belongs on a finished Mana engine, not a developing one, because the triggered spell still has to be paid for.

MAIN ITEMS TO CONSIDER

Endgame slots and route choices

The item plan revolves around two legitimate chest directions. Morior Invictus covers socket and defence value; a high-ES + %Mana rare chest pushes the raw-Mana ceiling. The surrounding slots reinforce Mana, recovery, crit or the rune engine.

Helmet note: a large pure-ES helmet is the default raw-Mana route under Eldritch Battery. The premium Jiquani package amplifies that slot even further, but the base guide remains functional without it.
