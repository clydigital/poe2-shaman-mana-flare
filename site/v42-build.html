Core idea

Make every Mana point work twice.

Maximum Mana is the shared resource connecting Mana Flare base damage, Archmage gain, Arcane Intensity, EB conversion and eventually Rathpith-style scaling. The build fails when trigger speed outruns Mana or Rage recovery.

1 · Carrier spellEntangle, Frost Darts or Orb supplies repeated eligible crit hits.
2 · Mana FlareConsumes 25% current Mana when triggered. Current Mana matters, not only maximum Mana.
3 · ArchmageAdds Lightning based on maximum Mana, making Elemental/generic scaling increasingly valuable.
4 · RecoveryRemnants, regen, flasks and overflow refill the pool before the next cooldown window.
5 · Rage engineFurious Wellspring feeds Druidic Champion. Do not let Rageforged starve the loop.

Progression

What to do next.

The progression path is intentionally recovery-first. A theoretical 1.5+ Flare/s is not useful if each proc empties the resource pool or Furious Wellspring cannot pay the Rage bill.

NOW · 49–60
Finish permanent rewards, cap elemental resistances, keep EB + Dream Fragments.

Archmage stays the Spirit priority. Use Crest/Waveshaper/high-ES rares as practical stepping stones. Do not buy extreme CDR yet.

52+
Test Strugglescream as a four-instil platform.

Compare one excellent notable against all four. Avoid spending an instil on a notable already allocated unless you are explicitly comparing its raw standalone value.

60–70
Decide whether native Entangle or Frost Darts is becoming real damage.

If a +skill wand + Focus package reaches the low/mid 30s cheaply, the wand hybrid becomes much more compelling because you retain the offhand.

75+
Compare cultivated Rathpith against +levels Focus / defence Focus.

Rathpith is the pure Mana-Flare branch. High-level Entangle/Frost is the hybrid branch. Staff remains a test route, but automatically sacrifices the offhand.

80+
Only then push luxury CDR.

Temporalis, CDR jewels and aggressive cooldown stacking belong after current-Mana recovery, Spirit, Rage and defence are already stable.

L30–36 hybrid skill lab

Entangle, Frost Darts and DD.

High gem levels do not benefit all three equally. Entangle and Frost become legitimate secondary damage engines; DD's corpse-life coefficient rises much more slowly.

Entangle · L36

Plant / Physical
~7.85×fissure vs L20
~9.56×vine vs L20

The strongest level-stacking transformation. Persistent vines also make it an excellent Mana Flare trigger-saturation carrier while dealing native damage between cooldowns.

Wand stretch route: Lv21 gem +5 Physical Spell wand +2 Focus +4 amulet +3 Prism +1 Physical Mastery = L36. A +2 Prism is already L35.

Frost Darts · L36

Cold / Projectile
~6.50×dart/chunk vs L20
~6.49×explosion vs L20

This is the boss-hybrid candidate. It starts with a higher base crit than Entangle and can create multiple eligible hit events, so high levels improve both native damage and trigger reliability.

Cold wand route mirrors Entangle: Lv21 +5 Cold Spell wand +2 Focus +4 amulet +3 Prism +1 Cold Mastery = L36 stretch target.

Detonate Dead · L36

Fire + corpse Physical
~3.47×flat Fire vs L20
~1.36×corpse-life coefficient

Shared levels are welcome, but DD does not justify the same expensive +skill package if corpse scaling is the main reason you use it. Treat it as wide corpse-chain cleanup.

Recommendation: keep DD cheap and let weapon/amulet levels it naturally shares be a bonus. Do not wreck the Mana or offhand package merely to force DD to 36.
Why L36 looks absurd: spell gems have steep late-level base-damage progression. That larger native base is then multiplied by the same Archmage / increased spell damage / crit / CDB layers. It is not a tiny “+16 gem levels” tooltip improvement; Entangle's native fissure base is roughly 7.85× its L20 value before the rest of the build scales it.
Separate front tab

Runeseeker clear / sell guide.

The objective is to unlock it, not build around it. Use the temporary 10k-current-Mana trick, obtain the item, then sell it unless the market/corruption gamble is unusually favourable.

1
Run Expedition / Logbooks

Progress toward Uhtred, the Stardrinker. Uhtred's Saga is the deterministic access helper when available.

2
Kill Uhtred for the Depleted Mana Rune

The rune is the character-specific quest item that starts the Runeseeker unlock chain.

3
Temporarily reach 10,000 current Mana

Overflow counts. You do not need a permanent 10k maximum-Mana build just to complete the unlock.

4
Use the Depleted Mana Rune at 10k+ current Mana

It becomes the Infused Mana Rune.

5
Give the Infused Mana Rune to Farrow

Receive Runeseeker's Call, then compare a clean sale with Runeforge/corruption risk.

Storm lab discoveries

Weird tech worth remembering.

These are the interactions that can change what you buy next. Anything unproven remains labelled as an experiment rather than silently becoming baseline DPS.

Multi-hit trigger saturation

BRRR ≠ no cooldown

More eligible crit hits make Mana Flare fire almost immediately when its cooldown becomes ready. Once trigger probability approaches 100%, more hits give diminishing returns and CDR/recovery become the next bottlenecks.

Fire Damage is live

Native Fire

Mana Flare's consumed-Mana hit starts as Fire. Archmage adds Lightning. Fire-only scaling therefore works, while generic/Elemental scaling can cover a larger share of the mixed hit.

Gain as extra

Another base layer

Extra Fire/Lightning/Cold/Chaos can add meaningful base damage. Treat the gained component as its destination type and do not recursively chain gains in the default model.

Strugglescream

1 vs 4 instils

Use the Instil Lab to compare the best single notable against all four. If a node is already allocated, Research should show its raw estimated contribution, while the equipped planner still prevents double-stacking.

Hybrid defence can preserve Mana

Armour/ES

Ruinic Helm or Spectral Ward can recover part of the Mana you give up by moving away from pure ES. The question is whether the retained mitigation/avoidance is worth the small raw-Mana loss.

Staff legality

2H = no offhand

Any staff test must automatically remove Focus/Rathpith/Crest stats. Ring slots, base skill gem state and support/Lineage state follow the same replace-not-stack rule.
