BUILD / CURRENT DIRECTION

Keep the old Mana Geyser idea. Strip the bloat.

Damage engine

Current Mana → Mana Flare Fire base → Archmage extra Lightning → spell/trigger scaling → crit + CDB. High-level Entangle or Frost Darts can become real native damage instead of existing only to carry the trigger.

Recovery engine

Mana Flare consumes 25% of current Mana. More CDR is only real DPS if regeneration, Remnants, flasks and recoup refill enough Mana between opportunities.

Defence engine

Pure ES gives the highest clean EB Mana. Armour/ES + Ruinic Helm and Evasion/ES + Spectral Ward trade some peak Mana for actual defensive layers.

Simple priority: Mana pool → Mana recovery → trigger reliability → crit/CDB → only then aggressive CDR.
PROGRESSION

What to grab while levelling

NOW · Lv49–60

Keep EB, 2× Dream Fragments, capped resistances where possible, Mana/INT/regen rares. Prioritise permanent campaign rewards before buying fancy CDR.

Lv50–60

Start comparing High ES versus Armour/ES and Evasion/ES pieces. Waveshaper becomes available at Lv51; Strugglescream at Lv52.

Lv60–70

Test the +skill hybrid route. Wand + Focus keeps the offhand; staff can push levels but automatically sacrifices every offhand stat.

Lv70+

Only push Rathpith/Temporalis/high CDR once recovery supports the resulting Flare frequency. Expensive cooldown with a collapsing current-Mana pool is fake DPS.

TRICKS / DISCOVERIES

Neat things that actually change decisions

Fire Damage is live

Mana Flare starts as Fire. Fire-only scaling affects the native component; Elemental/generic scaling hits both Fire and Archmage-added Lightning.

BRRR = saturation, not CD bypass

Repeated hit carriers make it very likely a crit is ready as soon as Mana Flare’s cooldown ends. Once that probability is near 100%, buy recovery/CDR instead of still more hit events.

Armour can protect DPS

If damage is being taken from Mana before Life, reducing incoming hits can preserve the same current-Mana pool used to scale the next Flare.

Ruinic Helm is a conversion trick

High Armour on the helmet is partially recycled into ES, then EB turns that ES into Mana. Hybrid Armour/ES is normally the sensible version of the trick.

Arcane Blossom is different

Mana Recovery Rate is not just another additive regen node. It scales recovery sources, making it especially interesting once regen/Remnants/flasks are already substantial.

CDR can be fake damage

If the calculator says sustainable Flare/s is below the cooldown ceiling, more CDR does nothing until recovery rises.

3 · DAMAGE

Why CDB, Fire, Lightning and Mana all matter differently

CDB

Critical Damage Bonus is the extra damage added by a crit. Expected crit multiplier is approximately 1 + critChance × CDB. At 42% crit and 191% CDB that is about 1.80× expected damage.

Damage types

Mana Flare’s native component is Fire. Archmage adds Lightning. Fire-only increases scale only the Fire component; Lightning-only scales the gained Lightning; Elemental/generic scales both.

Expected crit factor = 1 + CritChance × CDB\nRaw Mana Flare = 25% × CurrentMana\nGeneric scaled hit = RawCombined × (1 + additive increased damage buckets) × more multipliers × expectedCritFactor

5 · BRRR TRIGGERS

Multi-hit carriers saturate the cooldown

What works

Persistent/repeated eligible hits from Entangle, Frost Darts, Orb of Storms and similar carriers can make it highly likely that a crit is available immediately when Mana Flare comes off cooldown.

What does not

Ten crits during a 1-second cooldown do not automatically create ten Mana Flares. Multiple separate support instances should not be modelled as independent cooldowns without proof.

P(at least one crit in window) = 1 − (1 − carrierCrit)^(eligibleHitEventsPerSecond × cooldownSeconds)\nAt 42% crit and 4 eligible events in a 1s window ≈ 88.7%\nAt 8 events ≈ 98.7%
BRRR recipe: enough hit events to saturate → CDR → enough Mana recovery to preserve pre-Flare current Mana. More hits after saturation are mostly wasted for Mana-Flare throughput.
