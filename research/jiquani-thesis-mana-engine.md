# Jiquani's Thesis — Why It Is So Strong for Mana Geyser

## Role in the build

Jiquani's Thesis is an **aspirational / BIS-tier mana augment**, not a normal-budget recommendation. Its value comes from turning a high-Energy-Shield helmet into a second source of flat Mana on top of Eldritch Battery.

The interaction is strongest when combined with:

- a very high-ES pure Energy Shield helmet (for example, an Ancestral Tiara);
- Eldritch Battery;
- Darkness Enthroned with increased effect of socketed Augments;
- the build's existing % increased maximum Mana and Intelligence scaling.

## Why the interaction double-dips helmet ES

Jiquani's Thesis effectively grants **maximum Mana based on the Energy Shield on the equipped helmet**. With the high-augment-effect Darkness Enthroned setup used by extreme mana stackers, the effective conversion can approach roughly **+1 maximum Mana per 1 Energy Shield on the helmet**.

At the same time, Eldritch Battery converts the helmet's Energy Shield into Mana as part of the character's ES-to-Mana chassis.

So the same helmet ES is exploited twice:

1. **Helmet ES → Mana through Eldritch Battery**
2. **Helmet ES → additional flat Mana through Jiquani's Thesis**

A simplified 650-ES helmet example therefore looks roughly like:

- ~650 Mana-equivalent from the helmet ES through Eldritch Battery;
- ~650 additional flat Mana from Jiquani with the doubled augment-effect setup;
- ~1,300 raw Mana contribution before the build's wider maximum-Mana scaling is applied.

The exact final character gain depends on the order and buckets of ES, Intelligence and maximum-Mana modifiers, so the ~1,300 figure is an explanatory model rather than a tooltip guarantee.

## Why this changes helmet priorities

For the maximum-Mana version of Mana Geyser, a **huge pure-ES helmet becomes much more attractive than an Armour-heavy helmet**. Losing helmet ES can hurt twice: once through Eldritch Battery and again through the Jiquani conversion.

This means Runic Helm / mixed Armour-ES approaches should not be assumed superior. They need to beat the opportunity cost of a 600–700+ ES helmet feeding both EB and Jiquani.

## Budget warning

Jiquani's Thesis is currently extremely expensive and thinly supplied, so it should be treated as:

> **Aspirational BIS / proof of ceiling — not part of the normal budget progression.**

The practical build should first reproduce the rest of the mana engine cheaply:

- high local ES;
- Eldritch Battery;
- flat Mana;
- % maximum Mana;
- Intelligence;
- multi-socket / augment-effect gear;
- Bonded Mind-rune scaling.

Then Jiquani can be viewed as the final premium layer rather than something the build requires to function.

## Endgame takeaway

The reason extreme Mana Flare characters can reach apparently absurd pools is not only Runeseeker. Jiquani shows the broader principle:

> **Use one gear stat several times before multiplying it.**

Helmet Energy Shield is not merely defence or a single EB conversion. In the premium Jiquani setup it becomes another flat-Mana source as well, and the resulting enlarged flat pool is then amplified by the rest of the character's maximum-Mana scaling.
