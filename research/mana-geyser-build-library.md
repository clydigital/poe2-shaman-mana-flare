# Mana Geyser — Build Library Database

_Preserved from the final live guide data before the site was intentionally blanked on 2026-08-20._

This file keeps the useful build database without retaining the old UI/runtime.

## Build identity

- Shaman · Mana Flare / Entangle
- Mapping carrier: Entangle
- Boss carrier: Frost Darts
- Core Spirit engine: Archmage + Mana Remnants
- Stable flex: Eternal Rage
- Premium flex: Cast on Critical → Comet
- Long-term Mana target without Runeseeker: 7.5–10k+

Core loop:

1. Entangle or Frost Darts supplies crit-trigger opportunities.
2. Mana Flare consumes current Mana for its base hit.
3. Maximum Mana scales the wider Archmage / Mana engine.
4. Mana regen, Remnants, cost efficiency and flasks rebuild current Mana.
5. Furious Wellspring + Druidic Champion convert the Mana-regeneration chassis into Rage-supported spell damage.

## Main gems and supports

### Entangle — mapping core

- Mana Flare
- Eonyr's Thunder
- Magnified Area II
- Branching Fissures II
- Pinpoint Critical

Purpose: wide persistent coverage and frequent crit opportunities while moving through packs.

### Frost Darts — bossing core

- Mana Flare
- Concentrated Area
- Execute III
- Rakiata's Flow
- Pinpoint Critical

Rageforged II remains a possible replacement only when Rage supply can actually sustain it.

### Archmage — core damage Spirit

- Lightning Mastery
- Cannibalism I optional

### Mana Remnants — core recovery Spirit

- Remnant Potency III
- Harmonic Remnants II
- Khatal's Rejuvenation
- Clarity II
- Vitality II flex

Khatal adds cooldown value while Clarity contributes to sustained Mana uptime.

### Arctic Armour — defensive / retaliation Spirit

- Mana Flare
- Magnified Area II
- Cooldown Recovery II
- Pinpoint Critical
- Execute III premium

### Eternal Rage — default flex Spirit

Its value is the Rage generation itself rather than a mandatory five-link package.

### Cast on Critical → Comet — premium flex Spirit

- Comet
- Boundless Energy II
- Energy Retention
- Efficiency II

Use only after the Mana engine no longer dips badly under real combat pressure.

## Main item routes

| Slot | Item / route | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Main hand | Exceptional rare Wand | Prefer 2 sockets if possible. Prioritise Mana, useful caster damage/crit, then Perfect/Bonded Mind runes. |
| Offhand | Cultivated Rathpith | Endgame payload piece when it has the Mana-based spell-damage and crit cultivations. |
| Body route A | Morior Invictus | Socket/defence route. Best when Mana-per-socket, attributes and Bonded runes are doing real work. |
| Body route B | High-ES + %Mana rare chest | Raw-Mana route. Huge local ES + % maximum Mana can beat Morior for pure Mana. |
| Helmet | Huge pure-ES rare | Default raw-Mana choice under Eldritch Battery. Jiquani is aspirational, not required. |
| Belt | Waistgate → premium belt later | Strong progression sustain because it supports two Mana flasks and boosts flask recovery. |
| Rings | Dream Fragments → rare/Kalandra route | Dream Fragments is a strong bridge. Endgame wants flat Mana, %Mana, INT and regen together. |
| Amulet | Astramentis → Strugglescream | Keep Astramentis while requirements/INT matter. Swap when four instills beat the total loss. |
| Gloves / Boots | Exceptional socket rares | High ES/Mana/INT/resists plus extra sockets and augment effect. Shaman Bonded effects make these premium. |

Helmet note: a large pure-ES helmet is the default raw-Mana route under Eldritch Battery. The premium Jiquani package amplifies that slot further, but is not required for the base build.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Target | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Transition | 4k Mana | First clean permanent-Archmage breakpoint. |
| Mature | 6k Mana | Mana engine becomes the central priority. |
| Overflow gate | 6,667 max Mana | Roughly 10k current Mana at 1.5× overflow. |
| Recovery | 35–40% max Mana / sec | Repeatable recovery target as a share of max Mana. |
| Endgame | 7.5–10k+ Mana | Permanent Mana target without Runeseeker. |

Cooldown recovery becomes a real damage stat only after Mana recovery can support the higher trigger frequency.

# Progression Database

## Act 1 · Level 1–15

Goal: build the Mana / crit shell.

Suggested items:

- Rare Wand: Spell Damage / Crit / Mana
- Rare ES pieces with Mana or Intelligence
- Dream Fragments from level 12 if affordable

Priority: Mana, resistances and a reliable crit carrier matter more than expensive temporary damage.

## Act 2 · Level 16–30

Goal: turn on the real Mana-Flare engine.

Suggested items:

- Sands of Silk at 16 as a cheap bridge
- Serpent's Lesson at 22 only as an experiment
- Crest of Ardura at 28 for CDR + recovery
- Second Dream Fragments when it clearly beats the rare ring

Entangle / Frost Darts + Mana Flare begins to define the build here.

## Act 3 · Level 31–45

Goal: Eldritch Battery + real Mana stacking.

Suggested items:

- High-ES helmet/body upgrades
- Rare Wand with gain-as-extra / Spell Damage / Crit
- Visage of Ayah only while it is the cleanest EB enabler
- Nightscale / Maligaro's at 45 only if they beat a strong rare

Judge gear by final Mana + recovery + crit together rather than one isolated affix.

## Act 4 · Level 46–64

Goal: solve sustain before buying more CDR.

Suggested items:

- Lavianga's Spirits at 49 only for comfort
- Waistgate at 50
- Uhtred's Chalice at 50
- Waveshaper at 51 as a defensive bridge
- Strugglescream at 52 once four instills beat Astramentis

Permanent Archmage starts when current Mana remains healthy through a real fight.

## Early Atlas · Level 65–80

Goal: choose the endgame chassis.

Suggested items:

- Cloak of Defiance at 65 defensive route
- Adonia's Ego at 65 hybrid wand option
- Exceptional 2-socket rare Wand
- Cultivated Rathpith when the Mana cultivations are good
- Morior vs high-ES + %Mana rare chest: compare final Mana

Target: 4k → 6k permanent Mana while repeatable recovery climbs toward 35–40% of maximum Mana per second.

## Endgame · Level 80+

Goal: push 7.5–10k+ without Runeseeker.

Suggested items:

- Huge pure-ES helmet
- Exceptional augment-effect gloves/boots
- High-end Mana/%Mana/INT/regen jewellery
- Morior socket-max or high-ES rare chest
- Jiquani + Darkness Enthroned only as aspirational BIS

The finished chassis should remain coherent without Runeseeker; Runeseeker stays an economic/farming objective rather than the build foundation.
